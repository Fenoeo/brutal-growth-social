Brutal Growth Social Privacy Policy

Brutal Growth Social is a self-hosted web application used to manage and publish social media content through connected third-party platforms.

This Privacy Policy explains how Brutal Growth Social collects, uses, stores, and removes data when a user connects supported social media accounts, including TikTok.

Information We Collect

Brutal Growth Social may collect and store account identifiers, profile information, authentication tokens, uploaded media, post content, scheduling information, and other data required to provide publishing and account management features.

How We Use Information

Brutal Growth Social uses this information only to connect social media accounts, display connected account information, schedule content, upload media, and publish posts on behalf of the authenticated user.

How Data Is Stored

Data may be stored on infrastructure operated by the owner of this Brutal Growth Social instance, including application storage, logs, and databases required for normal operation.

Sharing of Information

Brutal Growth Social does not sell personal information. Data is only shared with connected service providers when necessary to perform requested actions, such as publishing a video to TikTok or uploading media to another connected social platform.

User Control and Data Deletion

Users may disconnect connected accounts at any time. Users may also request deletion of stored account data and related content by contacting the operator of Brutal Growth Social.
